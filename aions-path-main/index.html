About

Welcome to a smaller, slower kind of adventure.

Aion's Path is an independent cozy life-simulation RPG started in 2025 by Xyrn0, centered on village life, calm exploration, and meaningful personal choices. There is no need to rush: progress comes from time, effort, routine, and the quiet decisions that define where you belong.

This website is the central connection point for everything around the project: the playable alpha, the official wiki, development updates, screenshots, social links, and future community resources.

FAQ

Questions players may ask first.

What is Aion's Path?

A cozy pixel-art life-simulation RPG about village life, routine, exploration, and personal decisions.

Is the game finished?

Not yet. Alpha v0.8.8 is publicly playable, and features, balance, and content will continue to evolve.

Where can I play Alpha v0.8.8?

Download the current Windows alpha from the official itch.io page linked above.

Does the game have combat?

No. Aion’s Path focuses on daily life, professions, crafting, and exploration rather than combat.

Can I share feedback or report bugs?

Yes. The public alpha is still in development, and feedback is welcome through the game’s itch.io page.
